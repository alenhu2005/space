# Data and visual sources

- Earth texture: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, “Blue Marble”, visualization 2915. Credit: Reto Stöckli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA Earth Observatory.
- Moon texture: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, “CGI Moon Kit”, visualization 4720. Credit: Ernie Wright (USRA), Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC), and the LRO teams.
- Bright stars: [HYG Database v4.1](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/blob/main/hyg/CURRENT/hygdata_v41.csv) by David Nash / Astronexus, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The adapted subset keeps 523 stars of magnitude <= 4, excludes the Sun, preserves proper motion, and adds selected Traditional Chinese display names. This subset is also CC BY-SA 4.0; see HYG-LICENSE.txt.
- Astronomical calculations: Astronomy Engine by Don Cross, MIT License.

Distances and object sizes are exaggerated in teaching mode and are visibly marked as not to scale.

NASA original pages: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/ and https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/.
Curriculum: https://www.naer.edu.tw/PageSyllabus?fid=177 (107年11月發布版).
